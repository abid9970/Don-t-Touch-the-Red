HOW TO PLAY

🔴

NEVER TOUCH THE RED

Any collision with red orbs, sweeping laser bars, spinners, or gates results in instant destruction!

🔵

BLUE IS SAFE & POWERFUL

Collect Blue Energy Orbs for bonus points. Grab Blue Shields 🛡️ to survive a lethal hit, or Chrono Gems ⏳ to slow time.

⚡

NEAR-MISS GRAZE COMBO

Graze within inches of red obstacles without touching to trigger Near-Misses! Ramps your combo multiplier up to 10x!

⏱️

SPEED UP EVERY 10 SECONDS

Every 10 seconds of survival, the simulation accelerates into higher adrenaline speed tiers.

🎮

CONTROLS

Mobile: Touch & drag anywhere. Ball is elevated slightly above your finger for maximum visibility.
Desktop: Use Arrow Keys, WASD, or Mouse Drag.
